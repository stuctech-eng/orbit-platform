What is ORBIT

A cognitive scanner,
not a game with rules to memorise.

Cells drift across a black screen. A scanner sweeps past and, for a moment, one of them goes dark. Find it again once the scanner is gone — that's the whole game.

🧠

Memory

Trains object tracking and working memory — not reaction speed, not math.

⚪

Minimal

No ads, no scores flashing mid-round, no clutter. Just the scanner and your focus.

📈

Adaptive

An internal engine quietly follows your pace and keeps every round just challenging enough.
